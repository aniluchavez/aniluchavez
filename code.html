hippocampal-speaker-semantics

Ridge-regularized Poisson encoding models linking LLM contextual embeddings to single-neuron firing rates across speaking and listening. Core analysis for our Nature Communications paper on speaking and listening.

straighteningthenarrative

Trajectory curvature analysis of hippocampal population dynamics during conversation. Shows that curvature tracks semantic surprisal and is the dominant geometric metric aligning brain and LLM representations.

NeurIPS 2026View repo

ArtNavigation

Unity-based 3D virtual navigation paradigm integrated with intracranial neural recording systems. Designed for human patients in the EMU — with direct relevance to immersive BCI and prosthetic vision applications.

Iterated investment game for EMU patients probing social trust — designed to isolate neural correlates of trust formation and reputation tracking during intracranial recording sessions.
